## Problem

Cevdet's essays lived on an earlier site built with Zola, and short poems sat in a separate project that was never deployed. Writing needed one home of its own: separate from the professional portfolio, with a different audience, and readable, linkable and indexable piece by piece.

## Constraints

- A separate site, not a section of the portfolio, so personal writing does not mix with work for clients and employers. One links quietly to the portfolio, the portfolio links back from a Writing line, and both describe the same author in their structured data.
- English only, with every piece at exactly one URL.
- Every old article URL had to keep working after the move.
- Nothing went live before the essays were ready to publish.
- Brand colours and icons come from the shared logo registry, never typed by hand.

## Decisions

- **Start lean from the Astro Minimal Template.** One began as a local copy of the [starter](https://astro-minimal-template.cevdet.ch/) with everything it did not use removed, and each feature added back brought its own tests. The branded share-image pipeline returned once it was needed.
- **Content collections with strict fields.** Every entry needs a title, a description of at most 160 characters and a publication date. Drafts are filtered in one helper that every route uses, so a draft produces no page, list entry, feed item or sitemap entry.
- **Search-ready by default.** Every URL ends in a slash and has a self-referencing canonical. `WebSite`, `Person` and `Article` structured data match the visible content only. Each sitemap entry's `lastmod` is the piece's own updated or published date, list pages carry the newest of their pieces, and pages with no meaningful date carry none rather than a guess.
- **Redirects that preserve every link.** The old site is redeployed as a redirect map that sends each article, with and without a trailing slash, to its new URL with a 301.
- **Reading first.** Essays get a byline with a reading time, a reading-width body and a contents list built from native `<details>` that becomes a sticky sidebar on wide screens through CSS alone. The RSS feed carries full essay HTML.
- Icons, brand styles and `theme-color` are generated from the logo registry and committed, and the manifest is built from the same generated data.

## Results

- [one.cevdet.ch](https://one.cevdet.ch/) publishes Cevdet's essays and poems, with an RSS feed and a sitemap.
- Old article URLs and a short alias domain answer with a 301 to the new site.
- Every build runs Astro and TypeScript checks, unit and build tests and a deadhead audit of the output, in CI as well as locally.
